## Brand & Style

This design system powers a high-precision AI multimodal intelligence platform engineered for video creators, sound designers, and content analysts. The aesthetic fuses studio-grade precision hardware interfaces with hyper-modern digital telemetry. It communicates computational velocity, surgical acoustic fidelity, and predictive intelligence.

The visual style blends **Technical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- Translucent, backdrop-filtered structural modules create separation without adding visual bulk.
- High-contrast neon vectors ground complex spectrograms, scrubbers, and tokenized transcript data.
- Crisp boundary definitions ensure telemetry widgets, chapter arrays, and timeline visualizers remain actionable under intense data loads.

## Colors

The system uses three selectable operational profiles mapped through shared semantic tokens:

### Theme 1: Cyber Lime (Default / Field Telemetry)
- **Primary Accent:** Neon Lime (`#cbf946`) for active playheads, detected keywords, positive sentiment, and live render states.
- **Canvas Base:** Deep Obsidian Slate (`#0d1117`).
- **Surfaces & Containers:** Semi-transparent midnight carbon (`rgba(22, 27, 34, 0.72)`) with subtle specular rims (`rgba(203, 249, 70, 0.12)`).
- **Text & Telemetry:** Polar White (`#f0f6fc`) primary, Muted Zinc (`#8b949e`) secondary, and vibrant Lime (`#cbf946`) highlight cues.

### Theme 2: Midnight Nebula (Generative / Deep Studio)
- **Primary Accent:** Electric Violet (`#a855f7`) with Cyan Glow (`#06b6d4`) tertiary highlights.
- **Canvas Base:** Rich Abyssal Indigo (`#0f0c1b`).
- **Surfaces & Containers:** Deep violet-slate glass (`rgba(26, 21, 46, 0.65)`) with prismatic violet boundaries (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.18)`).
- **Text & Telemetry:** Iris White (`#f3f0ff`) primary, Soft Lilac (`#9d8ec2`) secondary.

### Theme 3: Studio Minimalist (Publishing / Daylight QA)
- **Primary Accent:** Deep Emerald Lime (`#15803d` to `#65a30d`).
- **Canvas Base:** Crisp Porcelain (`#f8fafc`).
- **Surfaces & Containers:** Bright frost tiles (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`) with neutral border delineation (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Text & Telemetry:** Obsidian Carbon (`#0f172a`) primary, Slate Gray (`#64748b`) secondary.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Processing / Neural Synapse:** Cyan (`#38bdf8`)
- **Action Required / Acoustic Spike:** Amber (`#f59e0b`)
- **Critical Audio Peak / Truncation:** Crimson (`#ef4444`)

## Typography

The typographic system separates syntactic data from navigational and analytical prose:

- **Display & Section Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, engineered posture. Always rendered with tightened tracking to emulate hardware silk-screening.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, high legibility grotesque with warm geometric curves to counter mechanical fatigue during long transcript analysis sessions.
- **Timestamps, Scrubbers, & Tokens (`Space Mono`):** Fixed-width baseline figures prevent layout jitter during realtime playback, frame-step adjustments, and millisecond scrubbing.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace uses a dense, responsive **Modular Studio Matrix** optimized for vertical timeline stacking and horizontal data correlation.

- **Desktop (1440px+):** Asymmetric 16-column grid. 3 columns dedicated to persistent media sources/navigation, 8 columns reserved for video telemetry/waveform canvases, and 5 columns dedicated to interactive transcript streams and insight feeds.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 12-column adaptive layout. The video/waveform engine locks to the upper stack; transcripts and AI insights shift to a tabbed lower quadrant.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column linear layout. Video previews stay pinned to the top safe area; waveform transforms into a micro scrub ribbon; AI transcript feeds render in a swipeable bottom sheet.
- **Component Padding Scale:** Built on a tight 4px base (`0.25rem`) to maximize high-density metric visualization without crowding interactive targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted atmospheric depth layers rather than drop shadows:

- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure chromatic void with low-opacity radial ambient gradients reacting to the active track artwork or video feed.
- **Panel Surface (Level 1):** Solid background with 70% opacity, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a `1px` subtle top-lit stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` in dark themes; `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` in Light Studio).
- **Interactive Scrubber & Cards (Level 2):** Elevated via localized neon back-glow (`0 0 24px rgba(203, 249, 70, 0.12)` for Cyber Lime; `0 0 24px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.16)` for Nebula).
- **Overlays, Menus & Tooltips (Level 3):** `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, hard edge borders, and zero ambient blur for clear separation over shifting waveform lines.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances rounded corners on contextual cards with pill profiles for system-state tokens:

- **Containers & Glass Trays:** Utilize structured medium rounding (`0.5rem` to `1rem`) to keep boundaries crisp alongside complex data graphics.
- **Interactive Badges, Timestamps, & Chapter Tags:** Styled as full pill capsules (`9999px`) to visually separate metadata tokens from surrounding data tables and text blocks.
- **Waveform Canvas Bars & Scrub Heads:** Waveform peaks feature soft-curved caps (`2px`), while the main scrub needle maintains a sharp, vertical line for microsecond alignment.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Modules
- **Primary CTA:** Solid accent color (Cyber Lime, Nebula Violet, or Studio Emerald) paired with saturated black typography. Zero border, high-contrast glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px var(--primary-accent-30)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Actions:** Translucent glass surface, `1px` structural outline, text matching theme-contrast white/black. Hover states trigger border luminance shifts.
- **Icon Actions (Play, Skip, Splice):** Precision square format with medium corner radius (`0.5rem`), transitioning background opacity on hover.

### Badges, AI Tokens, & Chips
- **Sentiment & Entity Chips:** Capsule pills holding monospaced telemetry tags. Negative sentiments flag with crimson borders, while key entities (e.g., speakers, topics) showcase low-opacity primary tinting.
- **Monospace Timestamps:** Borderless background badges using `Space Mono`, with micro letter-spacing to simplify scanning along the timeline.

### Audio Scrubber & Waveform Visualizer
- **Waveform Area:** Responsive canvas displaying frequency metrics. Unplayed regions render at 35% opacity; played regions glow with saturated primary color.
- **Scrubber Playhead:** A luminous vertical needle with a diamond-shaped handle and an attached sticky time-tooltip.
- **Chapter Dividers:** Hairline vertical indicators (`1px`) anchored with interactive pill tags on the top axis that expand chapter summaries on hover.

### Interactive Transcript Analysis Module
- **Transcript Segments:** Modular text blocks with clickable timestamps that sync playback to the corresponding video frame.
- **Live Sync Focus:** The active speaker block features a glass highlight treatment and a left-aligned primary color border accent (`2px`).
- **AI Keyword Highlights:** Subtly underlined with neon dotted accents; clicking launches an inline modal with semantic definitions and cross-video occurrences.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- **Filter & Search Surfaces:** Recessed, dark glass input fields with monospace placeholder styling (`Space Mono`). Active focus states shift the border to the accent tone with a soft neon blur.
